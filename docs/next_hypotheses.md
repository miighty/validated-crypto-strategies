The single next experiment is the compression filter on accepted breakouts because it has the best combination of mechanism, available data, speed, and direct continuity with today's evidence. Cross-sectional dollar-neutral L/S factor strategies (Amihud, funding-carry, residual-momentum, low-vol) have now failed four times running on this 30-coin universe — deprioritize further factor variants here in favor of items 1-3 (single-asset/single-mechanism ideas) or a fundamentally larger universe.

**2026-08-29 update:** a genuinely new calendar-seasonality hypothesis (weekend liquidity-withdrawal effect: go flat Sat 00:00 UTC -> Mon 00:00 UTC on BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP) was tested and REJECTED decisively — lost to buy-and-hold on all 4 assets, all 3 partitions, and the underlying mechanism (weekend price action being uniformly lower-quality) did not hold empirically (ETH/SOL weekend legs actually outperformed their own weekday legs). See [docs/WEEKEND_EFFECT_VALIDATION.md](WEEKEND_EFFECT_VALIDATION.md). Do not retest this exact calendar rule without a fundamentally different execution design (partial de-risking, longer delay) and a new rationale.
