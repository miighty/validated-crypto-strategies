The single next experiment is the compression filter on accepted breakouts because it has the best combination of mechanism, available data, speed, and direct continuity with today's evidence. Cross-sectional dollar-neutral L/S factor strategies (Amihud, funding-carry, residual-momentum, low-vol) have now failed four times running on this 30-coin universe — deprioritize further factor variants here in favor of items 1-3 (single-asset/single-mechanism ideas) or a fundamentally larger universe.

**2026-08-29 update:** a genuinely new calendar-seasonality hypothesis (weekend liquidity-withdrawal effect: go flat Sat 00:00 UTC -> Mon 00:00 UTC on BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP) was tested and REJECTED decisively — lost to buy-and-hold on all 4 assets, all 3 partitions, and the underlying mechanism (weekend price action being uniformly lower-quality) did not hold empirically (ETH/SOL weekend legs actually outperformed their own weekday legs). See [docs/WEEKEND_EFFECT_VALIDATION.md](WEEKEND_EFFECT_VALIDATION.md). Do not retest this exact calendar rule without a fundamentally different execution design (partial de-risking, longer delay) and a new rationale. Items 1-3 above remain the highest-priority untested single-asset/single-mechanism ideas.

**2026-08-30 update:** a genuinely new intraday-timing hypothesis (US cash-equity session return concentration: long only [13:00,21:00) UTC daily on BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP, flat otherwise) was tested and REJECTED — but for a cost-structural reason, not disproof of the underlying mechanism. Daily enter/exit (~3,000-3,129 trades/asset over the full history) at 30bps round-trip cost consumed ~939% of capital via costs alone, swamping the test before the session-return mechanism itself could be assessed. See [docs/US_SESSION_EFFECT_VALIDATION.md](US_SESSION_EFFECT_VALIDATION.md). Do not retest a daily full-flip implementation of this idea; if revisited, use a much lower-frequency mechanism (weekly/monthly partial exposure tilt) or a purely descriptive return-decomposition study first. Items 1-3 above remain the highest-priority untested single-asset/single-mechanism ideas.

**2026-08-30 update (small/mid-cap Amihud):** the already-REJECTED top-30 Amihud illiquidity study was retested on a genuinely thinner 54-coin universe (CoinGecko rank 100-400, cross-referenced against live Binance spot USDT-tradable pairs) — same mechanism/parameters, different universe segment. Result: Sharpe 1.43, beats cash/random control decisively, no walk-forward decay, survives doubled costs and 1-day delay, cross-sectional Monte Carlo p=0.0000 — the strongest cross-sectional result in this program to date. However Deflated Sharpe fails (p=0.121) at the program's true search size (n=84 backtests). Verdict: **PROMISING BUT INCONCLUSIVE** — do not deploy on DSR grounds alone, do not discard either. See [docs/AMIHUD_ILLIQUIDITY_SMALLCAP_VALIDATION.md](AMIHUD_ILLIQUIDITY_SMALLCAP_VALIDATION.md). Recommended next step if revisited: cross-exchange replication (Bybit/OKX, same 54-coin universe, subject to listing availability) before any capital allocation decision. Items 1-3 above remain the highest-priority untested single-asset/single-mechanism ideas; this near-miss also deserves a dedicated cross-exchange follow-up pass per the skill's own near-miss discipline.
